Privacy

Privacy, in plain English.

Last updated 24 October 2026.

bCalX (bcalx.com) is run by THE BITCOIN CORPORATION LTD, a company registered in England and Wales, company number 16735102 ("we"). This page says what we read, what we keep and how to remove it.

What we read

What we keep

  • Nothing from your file. It is read inside your browser and kept on your device until you clear it.
  • A short-lived cookie that keeps your wallet connected. No advertising or tracking cookies.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Your records on Bitcoin

If you choose to mint, each record is written to the Bitcoin SV blockchain, encrypted with your own wallet keys so only your wallet can open it. You can make any one public later, which puts it on chain in clear, signed by you. Anything written to a blockchain is permanent and can't be deleted by us or anyone else, so only mint what you want to keep.

Who else is involved

  • bWalletX, our own wallet, which holds your keys on your device.
  • Vercel, which hosts this website.

How to delete

  • Imported items live in this browser: clear the site's data in your browser settings to remove them.
  • Questions: support@bwalletx.com, or ask in Ask b inside bWalletX.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, and complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). Contact us first.
