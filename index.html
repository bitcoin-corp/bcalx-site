Host a Space

Put your Space on the calendar

Spaces are scheduled inside a room on bChatX, signed in with bWalletX. Once a Space is confirmed, it shows up here with its own page and share card.

  1. Open your room on bChatXSign in with bWalletX and go to the room you want to talk in.
  2. Room owners: Schedule a SpaceOwners and moderators pick a time, a title and guests. It’s free and goes straight on the calendar.
  3. Members: Book the SpaceAsk the room for a slot. The price is the room’s own rate by the minute (£1 an hour unless the room sets another, and it can be free).
  4. Approved, then confirmedWhen the room approves, pay a 10% deposit within 24 hours to confirm. The rest is paid before you go live. Both go to the room owner.
